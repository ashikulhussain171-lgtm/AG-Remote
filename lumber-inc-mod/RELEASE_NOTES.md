Lumber Inc 1.13.3 (com.lumber.inc, arm64-v8a) — Panther Mods menu

A "Panther Mods loaded" toast shows at start; a small "P" bubble floats on the left (its own window above the game; drag to move, tap to open). Every switch starts OFF; with all OFF the game runs unmodified code.

- Unlimited Currency: every currency balance (cash, Diamond, BizPoint, Reputation, VillageCoin, Gear, event coins) is held at 1e236.
- Free Upgrades / Level Up: every resource cost counts as affordable; construction materials and supervisor cards are not used up.
- Max Level (1 tap): one upgrade tap jumps that upgrade straight to its final level (machine cash income / speed and every other Upgradable_v2 upgrade).
- No Ads: rewarded-video rewards are granted without playing an ad; interstitials and banners are off.

Switch states are remembered. Balances already raised to 1e236 stay in the save after switching Unlimited Currency off.

Install: uninstall the original game first (signature differs), then install `Lumber.Inc_1.13.3_PantherMenu.apks` with SAI (Split APKs Installer).
